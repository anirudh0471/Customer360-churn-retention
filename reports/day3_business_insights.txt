# Customer 360 — Day 3 Business Insights

## Analysis: Customer Churn by Contract Type

### Key Findings
- Month-to-month: 3,875 customers, 1,655 churned, 42.71% churn rate.
- One year: 1,473 customers, 166 churned, 11.27% churn rate.
- Two year: 1,695 customers, 48 churned, 2.83% churn rate.

### Business Insight
Month-to-month customers have the highest observed churn rate. Two-year contract customers have the lowest observed churn rate.

### Recommendation
Investigate the reasons for month-to-month customer churn. Test targeted retention offers, improved onboarding, and incentives for longer-term contracts.

### Important Limitation
These results show an association between contract type and churn. They do not prove that contract duration directly causes customers to leave or stay.
