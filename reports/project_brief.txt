# Customer 360: Churn & Retention Intelligence

## 1. Business problem
A fictional telecommunications company wants to understand customer churn and identify opportunities to improve retention.

## 2. Project objective
Analyze customer demographics, subscription details, contract types and billing information to identify patterns associated with churn.

## 3. Key business questions
1. What is the overall churn rate?
2. Which customer segments have the highest churn rates?
3. How are contract types and payment methods associated with churn?
4. How much monthly revenue is associated with customers who have churned?
5. Which retention actions should the company prioritize for further testing?

## 4. Planned tools
- Python and pandas: data inspection, cleaning and exploratory analysis.
- SQL: structured queries and business metric calculations.
- Power BI: interactive dashboard and business reporting.
- GitHub: version control and project documentation.

## 5. Planned deliverables
- Documented and validated dataset.
- SQL analysis scripts.
- Python exploratory analysis.
- Interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Business findings and actionable recommendations.
- Reproducible GitHub repository.

## 6. Data limitations
The initial dataset is an IBM sample describing a fictional telecommunications business. Findings will be presented as sample-data analysis, not verified results from a real employer.

## 7. Success criteria
The project should produce reproducible calculations, validated data, clearly defined metrics, meaningful visualizations and recommendations supported by the analysis.
